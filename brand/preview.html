Variant Readability & Application Recommendations

Variant 16px Readability Contrast & Weight Recommended Application
(a) Outline Mark Moderate. At 16px the 0.88px inner gap between the rupee bars begins to soften on standard resolution screens. High contrast (16.8:1 white on maroon). Crisp at 24px and above. Primary App Header Topbar, Sidebar lockup (28px–32px), Hero states, Record empty state.
(b) Filled Bubble Knockout Excellent. The solid silhouette ensures unmistakable recognition even in 16×16 browser tabs. Maximum silhouette density. Rupee cutout punches through sharply. Favicon & App Icon Browser favicon (16px, 32px), touch icons, desktop app shortcuts.
(c) Two-Tone Mark Fair. The magenta accent on maroon background drops contrast to ~3.2:1 for fine details at 16px. White bubble is prominent, but dark magenta inner stroke is subtle against deep maroon. Marketing pages, large hero graphics (64px+), presentation decks.
